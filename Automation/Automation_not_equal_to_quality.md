## Automation is not equal to Quality.

Automating everything does not guarantee that your product is reliable.


Automation is a powerful tool, but **quality is a strategy**.
Here’s why:

- Poorly designed automation multiplies bad tests faster.
- Automated scripts can confirm functionality but not usability.
- They can’t replace human judgment, exploratory thinking, or risk analysis.
- True quality involves collaboration, clarity, and prevention — not just execution.
- Automation helps speed up delivery, but strategy gives direction.
- You can have 10,000 automated tests and still **miss the most critical defect if your mindset isn’t focused on quality**.
