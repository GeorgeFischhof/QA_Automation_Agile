# SENIOR QA vs JUNIOR QA  
*Different Titles. Different Impact.*

---

## JUNIOR QA  
**Learns & Executes**

### Focus  
Learns the application and business flows.

### Responsibilities  
Executes test cases and reports defects.

### Approach  
Validates assigned features and finds bugs.

### Dependency  
Relies on guidance from senior team members.

### Collaboration  
Works within the team and follows instructions.

### Impact  
Focuses on finding bugs and ensuring features work.

---

## SENIOR QA  
**Leads & Drives Quality**

### Focus  
Understands the product from the customer’s perspective.

### Responsibilities  
Designs test strategies and identifies risks early.

### Approach  
Challenges requirements before development and thinks beyond defects.

### Dependency  
Mentors junior testers and promotes quality across the team.

### Collaboration  
Collaborates with developers, product managers, and stakeholders.

### Impact  
Thinks about customer experience, performance, security, usability, automation, scalability, and business impact.

---

## SENIOR QA THINKS:
- Does this feature work?  
- Should this feature work this way?  
- What could fail in production?  
- How will customers use it?  
- Can we prevent defects instead of just finding them?  

---

## THE JOURNEY  
From Junior QA to Senior QA isn't about waiting for promotions — it's about continuous learning, taking ownership, improving communication, and becoming a trusted quality advocate.

---

⭐ **Experience earns you years, but ownership earns you the title of Senior QA.**
