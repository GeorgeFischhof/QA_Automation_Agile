## QA must understand the business — not just the code.

A great QA doesn’t only test features; they test value.
 
Knowing how the system works is important, but understanding why it exists changes everything.

When QA understands the business:
* Test cases reflect real user behavior, not just technical flow.
* Risk analysis becomes strategic — focused on what impacts revenue or customer trust.
* Communication with Product and Dev becomes faster and clearer.
* Bugs are not just “defects” — they’re barriers to business goals.

QA is not a detached tester; QA is part of the product’s success strategy.

Because quality is not only about making code work — it’s about making the business work.
